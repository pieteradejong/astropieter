# Raspberry Pi 5 Kit photos

CanaKit Raspberry Pi 5 starter kit, photographed after unboxing on 25 Aug 2026. These are the six frames emailed as `IMG_2822`–`IMG_2827`.

Image files live in [`public/projects/raspberry-pi-5/`](../public/projects/raspberry-pi-5/).

## Kit overview

![Full CanaKit Raspberry Pi 5 kit laid out on the floor](/projects/raspberry-pi-5/kit-overview.jpg)

**File:** `kit-overview.jpg` (`IMG_2822`)

Top-down shot of the whole kit on a white shipping bag. Visible items:

- Official red-and-white **Raspberry Pi 5** box, labeled **4GB RAM**, open
- The green **Pi 5 board** out of the box (USB, Ethernet, dual micro-HDMI, GPIO)
- Official **Raspberry Pi Active Cooler** (aluminum heatsink + black fan) and its small white box
- Black **CanaKit Case for Raspberry Pi 5** in clear packaging, plus the yellow/blue retail box
- White **CanaKit 45W USB-C** wall adapter still in plastic, plus its yellow/blue box
- **USB MicroSD card reader** on a CanaKit instruction card (note about pre-programmed cards)
- Coiled black **HDMI** cable
- CanaKit **GPIO Header Quick Reference** card

## Raspberry Pi 5 board (top)

![Close-up of the Raspberry Pi 5 board, top side](/projects/raspberry-pi-5/pi5-board-top.jpg)

**File:** `pi5-board-top.jpg` (`IMG_2825`)

Close-up of the board on the same white bag. Markings you can read:

- **Raspberry Pi 5** silkscreen and raspberry logo
- **Broadcom 2712** (`2712ZPKFSB00DOT`) under the metal heat spreader
- **RP1-C0** I/O controller
- RAM package with an **8G** silkscreen grid next to it (the retail box says 4GB)
- **HAT+ GPIO INTERFACE** 40-pin header
- Two USB 2.0 (black), two USB 3.0 (blue), Gigabit Ethernet
- Dual micro-HDMI (**HDMI0**, **HDMI1**), USB-C **PWR**, **BAT** and fan headers
- **CAM/DISP 0** and **CAM/DISP 1**
- **PCIe** connector, **Made in the UK**
- Edge of the silver active-cooler heatsink at the top of the frame

## GPIO reference card

![CanaKit Raspberry Pi GPIO Header Quick Reference card](/projects/raspberry-pi-5/gpio-reference.jpg)

**File:** `gpio-reference.jpg` (`IMG_2824`)

CanaKit **Raspberry Pi GPIO Header Quick Reference**. 40 pins, odds on the left (1–39), evens on the right (2–40). Color key as printed:

- 3.3V (orange) on 1 and 17; 5V (red) on 2 and 4
- GND (black) on 6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34, 39
- I2C (blue): SDA / GPIO2 on 3, SCL / GPIO3 on 5
- UART (gold): TXD / GPIO14 on 8, RXD / GPIO15 on 10
- SPI (purple): MOSI 19, MISO 21, SCLK 23, CE0 24, CE1 26
- PCM on 12, 35, 38, 40
- ID EEPROM on 27 and 28
- Remaining pins as GPIO

Links on the card: `support.canakit.com` and `canakit.com/quick-start/pi`.
